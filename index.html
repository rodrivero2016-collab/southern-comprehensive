How It Works

Three steps. No pressure at any of them.

  1. We talk

    A free call or a sit‑down. You tell me your doctors, your prescriptions, your budget and what you're worried about. I ask questions. Nothing is sold in this step.

  2. I compare and explain

    I bring back real options from the carriers I represent, side by side, with the trade‑offs in plain language — including the ones that don't favor me.

  3. You decide, and I stay

    You pick. I handle the paperwork. Then I check in during the year and review your plan every fall, because plans change and so does your health.
