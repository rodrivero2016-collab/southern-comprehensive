What I believe about this work

Insurance is one of the few things people buy hoping never to use. That makes it easy to sell badly and easy to buy badly. My whole approach is built on the opposite idea: if I take the time to actually teach you how this works, you'll make a good decision — and a good decision keeps you as a client for twenty years instead of one.

My mission

To make sure no Georgia family ends up with the wrong coverage, or no coverage, simply because nobody took the time to explain their options in language they understand.
