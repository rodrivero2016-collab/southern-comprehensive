What to bring

Having these ready means we can give you a real answer in one meeting rather than three.

  • Your Medicare card, if you have one
  • A list of your prescriptions with dosages
  • The doctors you want to keep
  • Your preferred pharmacy
  • Any Annual Notice of Change letter you received

No cost, no obligation

Our guidance is free to you. We're compensated by the carrier only if you choose to enroll, and your premium is the same either way. You can leave the conversation without buying anything and we'll still have done our job.

For Medicare beneficiaries

Booking a consultation does not enroll you in any plan and does not affect your current coverage. We will only discuss the plan types you ask about. To review every option available to you, including plans we don't represent, contact Medicare.gov, call 1‑800‑MEDICARE (TTY 1‑877‑486‑2048), or reach your State Health Insurance Assistance Program.
