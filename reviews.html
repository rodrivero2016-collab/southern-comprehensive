Read this before publishing anything on this page

  • Get written permission from every client before using their words or name.
  • Never invent, embellish, or composite a testimonial. Not once.
  • Testimonials that mention specific Medicare plans, benefits or carriers are marketing under CMS rules and require review before publication.
  • Avoid claims that imply guaranteed outcomes, savings amounts, or superiority over other agents.
  • Don't offer anything of value in exchange for a review — that creates both CMS and FTC exposure.
  • Keep the signed permissions on file. If a client withdraws consent, remove the review promptly.
