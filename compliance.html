How we handle marketing compliance

Medicare marketing is governed by the CMS Medicare Communications and Marketing Guidelines, which change annually, as well as by individual carrier and FMO requirements and Georgia insurance regulations. Our internal process:

  1. Classify the material

    Every public-facing item is first classified as either "communication" or "marketing" under current CMS definitions. Material that mentions plan benefits, costs, star ratings, or specific carriers is treated as marketing.

  2. Carrier and FMO review

    Marketing material is submitted to the applicable carrier or FMO for review and, where required, for CMS submission through HPMS, before publication.

  3. Required disclaimers applied

    The multi-plan disclaimer, the government non-affiliation statement, and any carrier-specific language are applied before anything goes live.

  4. Retention

    Approved copies, approval correspondence, approval identifiers and publication dates are retained per carrier and CMS retention requirements.

  5. Annual re-review

    The entire site is re-reviewed each year before the Annual Enrollment Period against the current plan-year guidelines, and dated disclosures are updated.

  6. Prompt correction

    Any item identified as non-compliant is taken down or corrected immediately, and the correction is documented.

Scope of contact

When you contact us about Medicare, we will discuss only the plan types and products you ask about. We do not make unsolicited contact in violation of CMS rules, and we obtain the required permission to contact and scope of appointment documentation before any Medicare sales appointment.
